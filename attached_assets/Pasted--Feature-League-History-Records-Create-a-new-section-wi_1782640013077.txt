## Feature: League History & Records

Create a new section within **Club → League Ladders** called **History & Records**.

This should become the archive for every completed season.

### Season History

At the top of the page include a season selector.

Example:

2026 ▼

Users can switch between every completed season (2026, 2027, 2028...)

Selecting a season loads the final standings for that year.

Display the complete final ladder for both:

* Senior World Beach Pro Series
* Youth World Beach Pro Series

Each ladder should include:

* Final Rank
* Club Logo
* Club Name
* Played
* Won
* Lost
* Set Difference
* Points
* Final Position

Highlight the user's club.
