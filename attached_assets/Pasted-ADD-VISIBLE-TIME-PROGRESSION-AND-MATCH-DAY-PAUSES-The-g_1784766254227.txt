ADD VISIBLE TIME PROGRESSION AND MATCH-DAY PAUSES

The game date advances, but it does not feel as though the season is moving forward.

Improve the sense of time passing without redesigning the existing calendar or time controls.

IMPORTANT:
- Preserve the existing Pause, Slow, Medium, Fast and Advance controls.
- Normal calendar progression should continue automatically unless the player pauses it.
- Do not interrupt the player for every AI-controlled fixture.
- Existing saves must receive these improvements.

1. VISUALLY MARK TIME ON THE ANNUAL CALENDAR

Every calendar date must have one of these visual states:

PAST DAY
- Slightly faded or darkened.
- Events and coloured dots remain visible.
- Completed match days may show a small checkmark.

TODAY
- Strong gold or cyan border.
- Subtle filled background.
- Clearly display “TODAY” when the date is selected or hovered.
- Today must be immediately recognisable without searching the calendar.

FUTURE DAY
- Normal calendar appearance.
- Future event dots remain fully visible.

SELECTED DAY
- Visually different from Today.
- Do not let the selected-day styling hide the current date.

As time advances, the calendar must update automatically so completed days gradually become shaded. This should make the year visibly fill up as the season progresses.

2. ADD A PERMANENT NEXT-EVENT DISPLAY

Near the existing game date and time controls, display:

NEXT EVENT
Event name
Date
Countdown

Examples:

NEXT EVENT
Regional Match vs Bondi Breakers
3 days

NEXT EVENT
Contract Deadline
Tomorrow

NEXT EVENT
World Tour Match
Today

This must always find the next relevant event for the player’s team.

Prioritise:

1. Player’s team matches
2. Required manager decisions
3. Contract deadlines
4. Transfer deadlines
5. Facility completion
6. Financial events
7. Training and club events

Clicking the Next Event display should open that event or select its date on the Annual Calendar.

3. AUTOMATIC MATCH-DAY PAUSE

When the calendar reaches a match involving the player’s team:

- Automatically pause time.
- Do not allow the calendar to advance beyond the match without resolving it.
- Display a Match Day announcement.
- Do not automatically pause for unrelated AI-team matches.

MATCH DAY ANNOUNCEMENT

Display a clear modal or full-width notification containing:

MATCH DAY
Competition name
Player’s team vs opponent
Venue
Scheduled date
Weather summary if available

Buttons:

- Watch Match
- Simulate Match
- Match Preview
- Decide Later

“Decide Later” keeps the game paused.

Time may resume only after the match has been watched, simulated or otherwise resolved.

4. IMPORTANT EVENT PAUSES

Automatically pause for events requiring the manager’s attention, including:

- Player’s team match
- Contract expiry requiring a decision
- Transfer or registration deadline
- Player or staff offer requiring a response
- Serious injury
- Manager job offer
- Board ultimatum
- Required Olympic squad selection
- Required competition registration
- End-of-season review

Show a concise notification explaining why the game paused and what decision is required.

5. NON-BLOCKING EVENT NOTIFICATIONS

Do not pause for routine events such as:

- AI-team results
- Ordinary wage payments
- Minor facility progress
- Routine training completion
- General competition results

Instead, show a small notification and add it to the notification centre.

6. MAKE DAILY PROGRESSION VISIBLE

When the date changes:

- Animate the date briefly instead of silently replacing it.
- Use a short calendar-page flip, number transition or subtle highlight.
- Briefly show the new day and date.
- Keep the animation quick so Fast speed remains usable.

Example:

MONDAY
18 MARCH 2027

At Fast speed, do not animate every individual date slowly. Use a faster transition and stop cleanly when an important event is reached.

7. UPCOMING EVENT WARNINGS

Provide advance warnings for the player’s team:

- Match in 7 days
- Match in 3 days
- Match tomorrow
- Match today

For important deadlines:

- Deadline in 14 days
- Deadline in 7 days
- Deadline in 3 days
- Deadline tomorrow
- Deadline today

Do not repeatedly show the same warning more than once per milestone.

8. DASHBOARD SEASON PROGRESS

Add a compact Season Progress section to the Dashboard showing:

- Current date
- Current competition phase
- Current round
- Next team match
- Days until next match
- Matches played
- Matches remaining

Include a small progress bar showing how far the current season has advanced.

Example:

SEASON PROGRESS

████████░░░░░░░░ 40%
Next Match: 3 days

9. TIME-CONTROL SAFETY

- Pause must always stop progression immediately.
- Slow, Medium and Fast must visibly show which speed is active.
- The selected speed must persist when navigating between pages.
- Opening a modal requiring a decision must pause time.
- Closing an ordinary information panel must not accidentally change the time speed.
- Never skip unresolved player-team matches or mandatory decisions.
- Resume the previously selected speed only after the blocking event is resolved.
- Do not resume automatically after “Decide Later”.

10. VERIFICATION

Test the following:

1. Start time progression on Slow, Medium and Fast.
2. Confirm the game date visibly changes.
3. Confirm previous calendar days become shaded.
4. Confirm Today moves to the correct calendar cell.
5. Confirm the Next Event countdown decreases.
6. Reach a player-team match and confirm time pauses.
7. Confirm the Match Day announcement appears.
8. Confirm the game cannot advance past an unresolved match.
9. Resolve the match and confirm time can resume.
10. Confirm an unrelated AI match does not pause time.
11. Confirm a contract deadline pauses when a decision is required.
12. Advance across a month and year boundary.
13. Confirm it works in existing saved careers.

Report all files changed and the events that now automatically pause time.
