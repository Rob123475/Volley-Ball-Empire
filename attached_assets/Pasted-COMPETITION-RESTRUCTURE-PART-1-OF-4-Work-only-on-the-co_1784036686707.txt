COMPETITION RESTRUCTURE — PART 1 OF 4

Work only on the competition structure and underlying competition data.

Do not redesign the UI yet.
Do not change Unity integration.
Do not change player images.
Do not change unrelated game systems.
Do not delete existing save data without a migration.

OBJECTIVE

Create the following competition hierarchy:

1. Continental Pool
2. Continental Regional League
3. World Tour
4. World Finals
5. Olympics in Olympic seasons

CONTINENTAL REGIONAL LEAGUE

There are six continental regional leagues:

- Europe
- Asia
- North America
- South America
- Africa and Middle East
- Australia and Pacific Islands

Each continental regional league contains:

- 6 active teams
- 2 players per team
- 12 active players
- 10 regional rounds

Each team must play every other team twice:

- 5 opponents
- 2 matches against each opponent
- 10 matches per team
- 30 actual matches per continent
- 3 matches per round
- 10 rounds total

Generate the regional fixture schedule using a proper double round-robin algorithm.

No team may:

- play itself
- play twice in the same regional round
- miss an opponent
- play an opponent more or less than twice

REGIONAL LADDER

Award:

- 3 ladder points for a win
- 0 ladder points for a loss

Sort the regional ladder by:

1. Ladder points
2. Matches won
3. Set difference
4. Match-point difference
5. Head-to-head result
6. Stable seeded tiebreaker only if still tied

After Round 10:

- Positions 1, 2 and 3 qualify for the World Tour
- Positions 4 and 5 remain in the regional league
- Position 6 is relegated to the Continental Pool
- The highest-ranked eligible Continental Pool team is promoted

CONTINENTAL POOL

Create a persistent pool for each continent.

Pool teams must be permanent teams with:

- stable team ID
- stable player IDs
- continent
- team name
- two-player roster
- rating
- form
- fitness
- fatigue
- pool ranking
- promotion history
- relegation history
- previous regional appearances
- previous World Tour qualifications

Do not generate a brand-new promoted team every season.

At season rollover:

- move the sixth-place regional team into its continent’s pool
- promote the top eligible pool team into the regional league
- preserve all player, team and historical records
- rebuild the next season’s regional fixtures using the new six-team lineup

WORLD TOUR QUALIFICATION

The top three teams from each of the six regional leagues qualify.

This produces:

- 18 World Tour teams
- 3 from each continent

Store the qualification source, qualifying position and season.

Do not implement the calendar or UI changes in this part.

VALIDATION

Verify:

- 6 regional leagues exist
- each has exactly 6 active teams
- each team has exactly 10 regional matches
- each continent has exactly 30 actual regional matches
- each round contains exactly 3 matches per continent
- every pairing occurs exactly twice
- top 3 qualification works
- sixth-place relegation works
- pool promotion works
- team and player histories survive promotion and relegation
- no console errors
- no duplicate team or fixture IDs
