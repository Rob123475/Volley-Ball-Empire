OLYMPIC ELIGIBILITY

Olympic national-team eligibility is separate from club employment.

A player may be selected for national duty whether the player is:

- signed to a regional club
- signed to a Continental Pool club
- listed in the player market
- a free agent
- unsigned
- otherwise unassigned to a club

Club employment must never be required for Olympic selection.

A country is eligible to enter Olympic qualification only when it has at least three eligible senior players of that country of origin.

Olympic players must represent their recorded country of origin.

- temporarily reference the existing player records
