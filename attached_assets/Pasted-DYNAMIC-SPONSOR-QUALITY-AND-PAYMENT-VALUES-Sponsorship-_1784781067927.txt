DYNAMIC SPONSOR QUALITY AND PAYMENT VALUES

Sponsorship quality and money must rise or fall according to each team’s performance.

Calculate a Sponsor Appeal Rating for every club using:

- Current-season ladder position
- Recent match form
- Continental Pool, Continental Group or World Tour level
- Finals qualification
- Trophies won
- Previous-season finishing position
- Club reputation
- Fanbase
- Media exposure
- Promotion or relegation
- Financial stability

Weight the current season and most recent completed season more heavily than older history.

SUCCESSFUL CLUBS

Teams that consistently win, reach finals, earn promotion or win trophies must receive:

- Higher-tier sponsors
- Larger signing bonuses
- Larger regular payments
- Better performance bonuses
- More Global and International offers
- More offers competing for their sponsorship slots

A major trophy or promotion should create an immediate improvement in available offers.

POORLY PERFORMING CLUBS

Teams that consistently lose, finish near the bottom, suffer relegation or lose reputation must receive:

- Lower-tier sponsors
- Smaller signing bonuses
- Smaller regular payments
- Harder or less generous contract terms
- Fewer International and Global offers
- More Local and Regional offers

Relegated teams should experience a noticeable sponsorship-value reduction at their next renewal.

CONTRACT PROTECTION

Performance changes must not rewrite the payment value of an already signed contract.

An active sponsor must continue paying the agreed amount until:

- The contract expires
- The club terminates it
- A clearly stated contract clause is triggered

When the contract expires, the club’s current Sponsor Appeal Rating determines the quality and value of its renewal offers.

BALANCING RULES

- Do not base offers entirely on permanent club reputation.
- A previously successful team that begins performing badly must gradually receive worse offers.
- A smaller club that performs exceptionally must be able to attract increasingly better sponsors.
- Use a rolling performance history so one loss does not destroy sponsorship value.
- Sustained poor performance must have a genuine financial consequence.
- Sustained success must produce a genuine financial advantage.
- Prevent unlimited financial growth by scaling offers within balanced minimum and maximum values for each competition level.
- Apply the same sponsorship-performance rules to user-controlled and AI-controlled clubs.

VISIBLE EXPLANATION

Each offer must show why it was generated, for example:

- Improved offer: World Tour qualification
- Premium offer: Continental champion
- Reduced offer: Relegated last season
- Local offer: Low current club reputation
- Increased value: Strong recent form

TESTING

Simulate clubs with different results and verify:

1. A championship-winning club receives better offers and more money.
2. A promoted club receives improved sponsorship offers.
3. A consistently losing club receives lower-value offers.
4. A relegated club loses access to inappropriate Global offers.
5. A struggling former champion gradually drops in sponsor quality.
6. A successful smaller club can climb through the sponsor tiers.
7. Existing signed contract values remain unchanged until renewal.
