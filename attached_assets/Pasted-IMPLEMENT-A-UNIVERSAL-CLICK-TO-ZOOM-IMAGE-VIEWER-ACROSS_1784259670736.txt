IMPLEMENT A UNIVERSAL CLICK-TO-ZOOM IMAGE VIEWER ACROSS THE ENTIRE APP.

GOAL:
Every full-size player, staff, scout and medical image must open in a large zoom viewer when clicked.

APPLY TO ALL EXISTING AND FUTURE IMAGES IN:

- Senior players
- Youth players, where an individual image exists
- Draft players
- Player Market
- Scouting
- Team and Roster
- Head Coaches
- Assistant Coaches
- Fitness Trainers
- Strength Conditioners
- Massage Therapists
- Promotional Managers
- Scouts and Super Scouts
- Doctors
- Medical Specialists
- Physiotherapists
- Nutritionists
- Sports Scientists
- Any player or staff profile, card, list, modal or detail page

REQUIRED BEHAVIOUR:

1. Clicking or tapping an image opens it in a centred full-screen lightbox/modal.
2. Display the complete original image without cropping or stretching.
3. Preserve the image’s original aspect ratio.
4. Allow the image to use up to 90% of the screen width and 90% of the screen height.
5. Add a dark semi-transparent background behind the enlarged image.
6. Add a clear X close button in the top-right corner.
7. Clicking the dark background closes the viewer.
8. Pressing Escape closes the viewer.
9. On mobile, tapping the image opens it and tapping outside closes it.
10. Change the mouse cursor to a magnifying-glass/zoom-in cursor when hovering over a zoomable image.
11. Do not navigate away from the current page.
12. When closed, return the user to the same page and scroll position.
13. Do not alter the underlying image files, image quality, cards or database records.
14. Do not create separate zoom code for every page.

IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT:

Create ONE reusable global image lightbox component and use it throughout the app. Update the shared player/staff/medical image components so existing images automatically gain click-to-zoom behaviour. Ensure newly generated player, staff and medical cards also receive this behaviour automatically.

IMPORTANT EXCLUSIONS:

- Do not make small icons, flags, logos, stars, menu icons, decorative backgrounds or buttons zoomable.
- Only the main portrait/card image belonging to a player, staff member, scout or medical employee should open.
- Do not redesign any cards or pages.
- Do not change navigation, menus, colours, spacing, text, filters or existing functionality.
- Do not add a separate page.
- Do not duplicate components unnecessarily.

ACCESSIBILITY:

- The viewer must be keyboard accessible.
- Give the close button an accessible label.
- Prevent keyboard focus from becoming trapped behind the open viewer.
- Restore focus to the clicked image after closing.
- Use appropriate button semantics for clickable images.

VERIFICATION:

After implementation, test the zoom viewer on at least one image from each of these groups:

1. Senior player
2. Draft or Market player
3. Scout
4. Team staff member
5. Doctor or medical employee

Also confirm:

- The image is not cropped.
- Escape closes it.
- Clicking outside closes it.
- The close button works.
- Mobile sizing works.
- No icons, flags or logos open in the viewer.
- Existing card actions and navigation still work.

Do not report completion until the universal image zoom works consistently across all listed sections.
