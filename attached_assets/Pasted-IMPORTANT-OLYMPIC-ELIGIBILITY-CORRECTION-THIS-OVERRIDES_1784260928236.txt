IMPORTANT OLYMPIC ELIGIBILITY CORRECTION — THIS OVERRIDES ANY CONFLICTING RULES IN THE PREVIOUS OLYMPIC PROMPT.

CHANGE THE NATIONAL ELIGIBILITY AND SQUAD RULES AS FOLLOWS.

==================================================
1. THREE REAL PLAYERS ARE MANDATORY
==================================================

A country must have AT LEAST THREE eligible real senior players of that country’s origin before it may:

- Enter an Olympic qualifier.
- Earn Olympic qualification points.
- Qualify for the Olympics.
- Enter the Year 4 Olympic tournament.

All three players must have the same permanent:

nationalityCountryCode

The nationalityCountryCode represents the player’s country of origin.

A country with only one or two eligible players is INELIGIBLE.

STRICT RULES:

- Do not generate a temporary third player.
- Do not generate an Olympic-only player.
- Do not use a player from another country.
- Do not borrow a player from another national team.
- Do not allow a staff member to fill a player position.
- Do not use club location as nationality.
- Do not allow a two-player country into qualifiers.
- Do not award qualifier points to an ineligible country.
- Do not reserve an Olympic position for an ineligible country.
- Do not manufacture players merely to make a country eligible.

There are now enough real players in the database. Olympic eligibility must be earned using the existing and naturally regenerated player population.

==================================================
2. PLAYER ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

For a player to count toward the three-player minimum, she must:

- Be a real senior player in the permanent player database.
- Have the country’s nationalityCountryCode.
- Be old enough for senior competition.
- Not be retired.
- Not be permanently unavailable.
- Not be serving a suspension covering the entire tournament.
- Not already represent another country.
- Have a valid permanent player ID.

The player may currently be:

- Signed to a club in any country.
- In the player market.
- A free agent.
- Signed to a foreign club.
- Playing in another continent.

The location and nationality of the player’s club do not affect national eligibility.

Example:

Brazil has three Brazilian-origin players:

- One plays for an Australian club.
- One plays for a Japanese club.
- One is a free agent.

All three remain eligible to represent Brazil because their nationalityCountryCode is Brazil.

==================================================
3. QUALIFIER ENTRY VALIDATION
==================================================

Before every Olympic qualifier, validate every country again.

A country may enter only if it has at least three eligible players of origin on the qualifier registration date.

If a country has fewer than three:

- Mark it INELIGIBLE.
- Do not include it in the qualifier draw.
- Do not create fixtures for it.
- Do not award it qualification points.
- Display the reason: “Requires three eligible players of national origin.”
- Move to the next eligible country where necessary.

A country that becomes eligible in a later year may enter the remaining qualifiers.

Example:

- Year 1: Country has only two eligible players and cannot enter.
- Year 2: A third eligible player enters the senior pool.
- Country may now enter the Year 2 and Year 3 qualifiers.
- It keeps zero points for the qualifier it missed.
- It may still qualify if its accumulated points are high enough.

Do not award backdated points for missed qualifiers.

==================================================
4. FINAL OLYMPIC QUALIFICATION VALIDATION
==================================================

At the end of Year 3:

1. Calculate qualification points from all three qualifying tournaments.
2. Rank countries within each continent.
3. Check that every prospective qualifier still has at least three eligible players of origin.
4. Lock only eligible countries into the Olympic field.
5. Replace an ineligible country with the next eligible country from the same continental standings.
6. Never exceed four qualified countries from one continent.
7. Never exceed 24 qualified countries overall.

A country cannot qualify based only on points if it does not have three eligible players.

Qualification requires BOTH:

- A qualifying position based on accumulated points.
- At least three eligible players of national origin.

==================================================
5. YEAR 4 REVALIDATION
==================================================

Revalidate all qualified countries before the Year 4 Olympic squad-registration deadline.

If a qualified country has fewer than three eligible players:

1. Search its existing real senior player pool again.
2. Include eligible free agents and players based at foreign clubs.
3. Do not generate a replacement player.
4. If three eligible players still cannot be found, withdraw the country.
5. Replace it with the next eligible country from the same continent’s final Year 3 standings.
6. The replacement country must also have at least three eligible players.
7. Preserve the maximum of four countries per continent and 24 countries overall.
8. Record the withdrawal and replacement in Olympic history and news.

If no eligible replacement exists within that continent:

- Leave the Olympic place empty.
- Run the tournament with fewer countries.
- Do not transfer the unused place to another continent.
- Do not create a fake country or fake player.

==================================================
6. NATIONAL PLAYER SQUAD
==================================================

Each country registers exactly three players:

- Two starting players.
- One reserve player.

All three must:

- Be real permanent players.
- Have the same nationalityCountryCode as the represented country.
- Have valid permanent player IDs.
- Be eligible for senior national competition.

The reserve may replace a starter because of:

- Injury.
- Fatigue.
- Suspension.
- Poor form.
- Tactical selection.

The three registered players temporarily join their national squad without leaving their clubs permanently.

After national duty:

- Return every player to her original club or free-agent status.
- Preserve her existing contract.
- Preserve injuries and fatigue.
- Add national appearances and results to her permanent career record.

==================================================
7. INJURIES AFTER SQUAD REGISTRATION
==================================================

A country must have three eligible players when entering qualifiers, qualifying for the Olympics and registering its Olympic squad.

If a registered player becomes injured during the tournament:

- Keep her registered with the national squad.
- Use the reserve when possible.
- Do not generate another player.
- Do not borrow a foreign player.
- Apply normal injury and forfeiture rules if the country cannot field two fit starters.
- An injury during the tournament does not retroactively erase qualification.

If a player becomes unavailable before the squad-registration deadline:

- Replace her only with another real eligible player from the same country.
- If no third eligible national player exists, apply the withdrawal and replacement rules.

==================================================
8. OLYMPIC STAFF MAY BE INTERNATIONAL
==================================================

Nationality restrictions apply only to PLAYERS.

Olympic and qualifier staff may come from any country.

National teams may hire or appoint international:

- Head Coaches
- Assistant Coaches
- Fitness Trainers
- Strength Conditioners
- Massage Therapists
- Promotional Managers
- Scouts
- Doctors
- Medical Specialists
- Physiotherapists
- Nutritionists
- Sports Scientists

Examples:

- Brazil may appoint an Australian head coach.
- Australia may appoint a Japanese physiotherapist.
- Japan may appoint a Brazilian sports scientist.

A staff member’s nationality must not:

- Affect the country’s Olympic eligibility.
- Count toward the three-player requirement.
- Change the nationality of players.
- Prevent the staff member from working for a foreign national team.
- Cause the national team to be marked ineligible.

Only the three registered players must be from the represented country of origin.

==================================================
9. NATIONAL STAFF APPOINTMENTS
==================================================

If national-team staffing is implemented:

- National appointments are temporary.
- Staff do not permanently leave their club unless normal contract logic says so.
- Prevent scheduling conflicts during national duty.
- National staff contracts or assignments end after the qualifier or Olympics.
- Staff experience and achievements may record national-team service.
- Staff nationality is informational only.
- Do not limit staff selection by continent or country.

If the game does not yet support national staff appointments:

- Use suitable existing staff-selection logic.
- Do not create a large new staff-management system unless required.
- Ensure staff nationality never blocks a national team’s participation.

==================================================
10. UI REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

In Olympic qualification tables, show:

- Country.
- Flag.
- Continent.
- Number of eligible origin players.
- Qualification points.
- Qualification position.
- Eligibility status.
- Reason for ineligibility.

Eligibility labels:

- ELIGIBLE — three or more eligible players.
- INELIGIBLE — fewer than three eligible players.
- QUALIFIED — earned a qualifying position and passed eligibility validation.
- WITHDRAWN — failed eligibility validation after qualification.
- REPLACEMENT — entered after a higher-ranked country became ineligible.

On the national squad screen, display:

- Three registered players.
- Country of origin.
- Current club.
- Starting or reserve status.
- Fitness.
- Fatigue.
- Injury status.
- Form.

Display national staff separately from players.

Never include staff members in the eligible-player count.

==================================================
11. REGENERATION AND FUTURE ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Player regeneration must preserve the country-of-origin system.

New regenerated players must receive:

- A valid nationalityCountryCode.
- A valid country of origin.
- A unique permanent player ID.
- A continent based on the country of origin.

Regeneration may naturally make additional countries eligible in future cycles.

However:

- Do not generate players specifically to rescue a country before a qualifier.
- Do not force every country to become eligible.
- Do not alter player nationality to fill national teams.
- Allow the global player population to evolve naturally.

==================================================
12. REMOVE CONFLICTING PREVIOUS LOGIC
==================================================

Find and remove or replace any previous rule that:

- Generates a temporary third Olympic player.
- Allows a country with two players to compete.
- Uses a two-player team plus a wildcard.
- Automatically fills an incomplete national roster.
- Allows a foreign player to represent the country.
- Treats staff nationality as an eligibility requirement.

Do not leave both the old and new eligibility systems active.

There must be one authoritative Olympic eligibility rule:

A COUNTRY MUST HAVE AT LEAST THREE REAL ELIGIBLE PLAYERS OF THAT COUNTRY’S ORIGIN.

==================================================
13. REQUIRED TESTS
==================================================

TEST 1 — THREE PLAYERS:

- Country with three eligible origin players can enter.
- Country with two eligible origin players cannot enter.
- Country with one eligible origin player cannot enter.
- No temporary player is generated.

TEST 2 — FOREIGN CLUBS:

- Three Brazilian players at clubs outside Brazil still make Brazil eligible.
- Their clubs do not change their nationality.
- They return to their original clubs after national duty.

TEST 3 — INTERNATIONAL STAFF:

- Brazil can appoint an Australian coach.
- Brazil remains eligible because its three players are Brazilian.
- The Australian coach does not count as a Brazilian player.
- Staff nationality does not block entry.

TEST 4 — LATE ELIGIBILITY:

- A country with two players misses Year 1.
- It gains a third real senior player before Year 2.
- It may enter Years 2 and 3.
- It receives no Year 1 points.

TEST 5 — LOSS OF ELIGIBILITY:

- A qualified country falls below three available players before Year 4 registration.
- No replacement player is generated.
- The next eligible country from the same continent replaces it.
- The total field remains at or below 24.

TEST 6 — TOURNAMENT INJURY:

- A country registers three eligible players.
- One player is injured during the Olympics.
- The reserve can enter.
- Qualification is not deleted retrospectively.
- No foreign or generated player is added.

TEST 7 — LONG-TERM SAVE:

- Eligibility remains correct after saving and loading.
- Player transfers do not change country of origin.
- Regenerated players use valid nationalityCountryCode values.
- No Olympics contains an ineligible country.
- No Olympics exceeds 24 countries.

DO NOT REPORT COMPLETION UNTIL THE OLD TWO-PLAYER/TEMPORARY-PLAYER LOGIC HAS BEEN REMOVED AND ALL TESTS PASS.
