SENIOR PLAYER STOCKTAKE — READ-ONLY AUDIT.

Audit every senior player currently registered in the authoritative player data system.

SCOPE:
- Senior players only.
- Include assigned players, free agents, market players, draft players and other unassigned senior players.
- Exclude all youth players.
- Exclude staff and medical staff.
- Exclude temporary/generated Olympic-only players.
- Count each permanent senior player exactly once.

STRICT RULES:
- Do not edit any JSON, database record, image, roster, team, competition or code.
- Do not delete, move, assign or generate players.
- Do not create teams or populate the draft.
- Do not repair problems during this audit.
- Report problems only.
- Use the authoritative player records as the primary source and reconcile them against the senior-player image directory.

PRODUCE THE FOLLOWING STOCKTAKE:

1. OVERALL TOTALS
- Total permanent senior-player records
- Total senior-player image files
- Players with working images
- Players without images
- Images without player records
- Assigned senior players
- Unassigned/free-agent senior players
- Players currently in the market
- Players currently in the draft
- Players assigned to more than one location

2. CONTINENTAL BREAKDOWN

For each of the six project continents, report:
- Total senior players
- Assigned players
- Unassigned players
- Number of nationalities represented
- Number of possible teams using the game’s existing team-size rules
- Players remaining after forming those teams

Use these exact continent groups:
- Europe
- Asia
- South America
- North America
- Africa/Middle East
- Australia & Pacific Islands

Use the project’s existing nationality-to-continent mapping. Report any nationality with a missing or conflicting continent.

3. NATIONALITY BREAKDOWN

Produce a table sorted by continent and then nationality containing:
- Nationality
- Continent
- Total senior players
- Assigned
- Unassigned
- Number of possible teams
- Players remaining
- Olympic eligibility

Olympic eligibility rules:
- Three or more permanent senior players from that country = ELIGIBLE
- Two permanent senior players = ONE SHORT
- One permanent senior player = TWO SHORT
- Zero players = do not include
- Do not generate the missing Olympic player during this audit.

4. OLYMPIC SUMMARY
- Total Olympic-eligible countries
- Eligible countries listed by continent
- Countries with exactly two players
- Countries with exactly one player
- Confirm that nationality is determined by country of origin, not club or staff nationality
- Do not count temporary Olympic wildcard/generated players

5. ROLE BALANCE

Report totals for only these approved roles:
- Spiker
- Blocker
- Defender
- Setter
- All-Rounder

Break the role totals down:
- Globally
- By continent
- Report any invalid, indoor or legacy role values separately

6. PLAYER PROFILE BREAKDOWN
- Players by star rating
- Players by age bands: 18–21, 22–25, 26–29, 30–33, 34–37 and 38–40
- Players younger than 18 or older than 40
- Average age by continent
- Average height by continent

7. DATA AND IMAGE INTEGRITY
Report:
- Duplicate player IDs
- Duplicate full names
- Duplicate image paths
- Multiple records using one image
- Missing image paths
- Broken image paths
- Orphaned senior-player images
- Records with mismatched filename nationality
- Records whose JSON name, nationality, age, height, role or stars conflict with the supplied image card
- Missing required JSON fields
- Invalid roles, statuses, nationalities or continents

8. FINAL CAPACITY SUMMARY
Using the current implemented competition/team-size rules, report:
- How many complete senior teams can be formed globally
- How many can be formed within each continent
- Whether each continent has enough players for its planned continental competition
- Exact surplus or shortage for every continent
- How many unassigned players could enter a draft pool without removing anyone from an existing roster

FINAL RESPONSE FORMAT:
A. Overall totals
B. Continent table
C. Nationality/Olympics table
D. Role and age breakdown
E. Integrity problems
F. Team-capacity summary
G. A final list titled “DECISIONS REQUIRED”

Do not make the decisions and do not change anything. Stop after presenting the complete senior-player stocktake.
