# Beach Volleyball Empire, v1.1.1

1.1.1 adds a Clubs page and changes how players move between clubs: a transfer window, buying ahead, one chance to keep a player another club wants, perks, and deals for the AI clubs' academy youths.

## The Clubs page
- **Every club in the game on one page**, yours included. Each club shows:
  - its badge, continent and tier;
  - its World Tour ladder and regional league positions;
  - this season's record and its last five results;
  - its balance, and its wages against the salary cap;
  - its injuries this season.
- **Sort the list** by richest, poorest, most successful, most injury prone, continent or name.
- **Open any AI club** for:
  - its players. Each senior is on her Player Market card, so you can scout her, watch her or make a bid.
  - its academy youths.
  - its staff and medical team.

## The transfer window
- **Players change clubs in the transfer window**: the Off-Season, 7 to 31 December. It's on the calendar.
- **Buy ahead.** Make a bid for a player at another club at any time of year.
  - Her club gets the fee the day the deal is agreed.
  - She joins you when the window opens, or the day after her contract ends if that comes first.
  - Until then her old club pays her. Her wage starts with you the day she arrives.
  - Your salary cap is checked for the day she arrives, so a contract that ends before then makes room.
  - The Player Market shows "Joins [club] on [date]", and the Team page's new Transfers tab lists who's coming and who's going.
- **No dumping.** Outside the window you can't release or sell a senior. Contracts that run out still end. Cover you signed for an injured player can go once she's fit again.
- The AI clubs buy ahead too, from each other and from you.

## One chance to keep her
- **When an AI club bids for one of your players**, you see its whole offer: the club, the fee, her wage, the contract length and any perks. You have 14 days to answer, and the deadline is on the calendar.
- **You get one counter-offer.** She weighs both: the wage over the contract, plus what the perks mean to her.
- **If the two offers are close, her mood decides.** A happy player at a winning club stays. An unhappy one at a losing club goes.
- If you say nothing, she goes and the fee is yours.
- **The AI clubs counter your bids the same way**, if they can afford it and it fits under their cap.

## Perks
- **Ten perks** you can add to a senior's contract, paid monthly with her wage and counted toward the salary cap:
  - phone and internet ($220)
  - gym membership ($270)
  - new car ($450)
  - clothing allowance ($540)
  - beauty salon ($670)
  - family flights home ($900)
  - personal media agent ($900)
  - private chef ($1,120)
  - personal trainer ($1,350)
  - beach house ($3,580)
- They show in Finances as "Player perks". When she weighs two offers, the big ones mean more to her than they cost.
- Add them when you sign, renew or counter. Contracts from before 1.1.1 have none.

## The AI clubs' youths
- **Ask any AI club for one of its academy youths, on loan or to buy, at any time.** Use the Clubs page: open a club and click "Loan or buy" on her card.
- **A loan** of 6 or 12 months is always agreed while her age allows it.
- **A sale is her club's choice, and your Scout does the talking.**
  - With a weak scout, or none, the club offers only a loan. Without a Scout, your best coach negotiates.
  - A club with youths to spare, or one that needs money, sells more readily. One that plays her in its youth team holds on.
  - The dialog shows your chance this week. The club's answer holds for the week.
- A youth you buy joins your academy, and her club is paid her youth price.
- The AI clubs buy each other's youths the same way.

## The calendar
New dates on the calendar:
- the Day 1 draft;
- the AI clubs' staff day on 8 January, with its reminder from 1 January;
- the new season;
- the pre-season choice day and your camp's dates;
- the transfer window opening and closing;
- players arriving and leaving;
- bids to answer;
- youth loans ending;
- your staff's contract ends.

Transfers have their own colour in the legend.

## Also
- The Rules page explains all of the above.
- Your saved careers carry on. Existing contracts have no perks, and the AI clubs start bidding in their next week.
