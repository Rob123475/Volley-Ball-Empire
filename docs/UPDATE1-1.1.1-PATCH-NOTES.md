# Beach Volleyball Empire, v1.1.1

1.1.1 adds a Clubs page and changes how players move between clubs: a transfer window, buying ahead, one chance to keep a player another club wants, perks, and deals for the AI clubs' academy youths.

## The Clubs page
- **Every club in the game on one page**, yours included. Each club shows:
  - its badge, continent and tier;
  - its World Tour ladder and regional league positions;
  - this season's record and its last five results;
  - its balance, and its wages against the salary cap;
  - its injuries this season.
- **Sort the list** by richest, poorest, most successful, most injury prone, continent or name.
- **Open any AI club** for:
  - its players. Each senior is on her Player Market card, so you can scout her, watch her or make a bid.
  - its academy youths.
  - its staff and medical team.

## The transfer window
- **Players change clubs in the transfer window**: the Off-Season, 7 to 31 December. It's on the calendar.
- **Buy ahead.** Make a bid for a player at another club at any time of year.
  - Her club gets the fee the day the deal is agreed.
  - She joins you when the window opens, or the day after her contract ends if that comes first.
  - Until then her old club pays her. Her wage starts with you the day she arrives.
  - Your salary cap is checked for the day she arrives, so a contract that ends before then makes room.
  - That room is spoken for: renewing the player whose contract made it has to fit under the cap on the day she arrives, even at the same wage.
  - The Player Market shows "Joins [club] on [date]", and the Team page's new Transfers tab lists who's coming and who's going.
- **No dumping.** Outside the window you can't release or sell a senior. Contracts that run out still end. Cover you signed for an injured player can go once she's fit again.
- The AI clubs buy ahead too, from each other and from you.

## One chance to keep her
- **When an AI club bids for one of your players**, you see its whole offer: the club, the fee, her wage, the contract length and any perks. You have 14 days to answer, and the deadline is on the calendar.
- **You get one counter-offer.** She weighs both: the wage over the contract, plus what the perks mean to her.
- **If the two offers are close, her mood decides.** A happy player at a winning club stays. An unhappy one at a losing club goes.
- If you say nothing, she goes and the fee is yours.
- **The AI clubs counter your bids the same way**, if they can afford it and it fits under their cap.

## Perks
- **Ten perks** you can add to a senior's contract, paid monthly with her wage and counted toward the salary cap:
  - phone and internet ($220)
  - gym membership ($270)
  - new car ($450)
  - clothing allowance ($540)
  - beauty salon ($670)
  - family flights home ($900)
  - personal media agent ($900)
  - private chef ($1,120)
  - personal trainer ($1,350)
  - beach house ($3,580)
- They show in Finances as "Player perks". When she weighs two offers, the big ones mean more to her than they cost.
- Add them when you sign, renew or counter. Contracts from before 1.1.1 have none.

## The AI clubs' youths
- **Ask any AI club for one of its academy youths, on loan or to buy, at any time.** Use the Clubs page: open a club and click "Loan or buy" on her card.
- **A loan** of 6 or 12 months is always agreed while her age allows it.
- **A sale is her club's choice, and your Scout does the talking.**
  - With a weak scout, or none, the club offers only a loan. Without a Scout, your best coach negotiates.
  - A club with youths to spare, or one that needs money, sells more readily. One that plays her in its youth team holds on.
  - The dialog shows your chance this week. The club's answer holds for the week.
- A youth you buy joins your academy, and her club is paid her youth price.
- The AI clubs buy each other's youths the same way.

## The Watch List and youth cards
- **The Watch List is easy to find:** a "Watch List" pill in the Player Market's top row, beside Senior Players and Youth Players, with how many you're watching.
- **Every card on the Watch List has three buttons:** Remove from list, Scout and Buy. Scout and Buy work exactly as they do on the market:
  - the same cost and 5 days;
  - the same two-at-a-time limit;
  - the same salary cap and transfer window.
- **Youths on the Watch List:** star any youth and she appears there, under a Seniors / Youths filter. A youth in an AI club's academy is bought through her club, as on the Clubs page.
- **Youth cards have a proper Scout button:** the same size and place as on a senior's card ($500, 5 days). They also get the Watch star, on the Player Market and on the Clubs page.

## Scouted players first
- **Every list puts the players and staff you've scouted at the top.** Next come those with a report on the way, then the rest. That covers both Player Market lists (seniors and youths), the Staff Market, the Medical list, the Watch List and the Clubs page.
- The Sort menu still orders each group.
- A card moves up the day its report comes in.

## Staff Market
- **A "Medical" pill** at the top of the Staff Market opens the medical hiring list. Everything else is where it was.

## Junior medics
- **37 new medical staff**, each with a portrait: 14 Registrars, 10 Physio Assistants and 13 Orthopaedic Surgeons.
- **A Registrar is a junior Doctor, and a Physio Assistant a junior Physiotherapist.** They're cheaper and less skilled, but they treat injuries the same way.
- **They're promoted to the full role within three years at a club,** with more skill and a full role's wage.
- **An Orthopaedic Surgeon** is a Medical Specialist who is a little stronger and a little dearer.
- They count under your staff budget, and the AI clubs hire and promote them by the same rules.

## Scouting: two reports at a time
- **Each Scout, Head Coach and Assistant Coach works on two reports at a time.** Players, youths, staff and medical all count.
- **Ask for a third and you're told who is busy**, and the day the next report is ready.
- **A Scout away on a youth scouting mission does no reports**, and can't be sent on a second mission, until the mission ends.
- **Asking an AI club to sell you a youth is a negotiation.** It takes one of your negotiator's two places for 5 days. A loan needs none.
- The Staff page shows each of your scouts' reports in progress, and any mission.
- The AI clubs work to the same limit.

## Your main sponsor and the staff budget
- **Your main sponsor sets your staff budget.** All your staff and medical wages together must stay under it.
- **A hire that would take you over is refused**, and you're told how much to free up.
- **Choose your main sponsor** on Day 1 (beside the draft) and at the start of every season, from three offers. Each has its own staff budget and its own bonus:
  - money for every win;
  - a share on top of your prize money;
  - a big payment for reaching the World Finals.
- **A new club's budget** covers a good head coach, a good medic and two average staff, but not the two best-paid staff in the game together.
- **The offers grow as you succeed.** Good World Tour finishes and money in the bank bring bigger offers, enough for several top staff.
- **Staff you already have keep their jobs.** But while you're over your budget you can't hire or renew anyone.
- **Where to see it:** Finances shows your main sponsor, its bonus, what it has paid you, and your staff wages against the budget. The Staff and Medical markets show the room left.
- The AI clubs choose a main sponsor too, and hire within their budget.

## The calendar
New dates on the calendar:
- the Day 1 draft;
- the AI clubs' staff day on 8 January, with its reminder from 1 January;
- the new season;
- the pre-season choice day and your camp's dates;
- the transfer window opening and closing;
- players arriving and leaving;
- bids to answer;
- youth loans ending;
- your staff's contract ends.

Transfers have their own colour in the legend.

## Also
- The Rules page explains all of the above.
- Your saved careers carry on. Existing contracts have no perks, and the AI clubs start bidding in their next week.
