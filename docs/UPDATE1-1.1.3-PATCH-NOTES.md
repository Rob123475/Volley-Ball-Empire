# Beach Volleyball Empire, v1.1.3

1.1.3 brings the Empire Nations Cup to the 3D court, lets you coach a nation, puts medal ceremonies on the podium, and adds real flags and new staff.

## Coach a nation
- **A national job on top of your club job.** When you have no national job, 1 to 3 nations offer you one: in the six months before a Nations Cup, and as soon as your national contract ends. The better your record, the better the nations that ask.
- **3 or 5 years, your only choice.** The pay is fixed: the weakest nations pay the most and the strongest the least. The pay is yours, on top of your club salary, and never touches the club's money.
- **The National Team page:** your nation's players at every club, with injuries and fitness. Pick your pair for the Cup (an injured player can't be picked; pick no one and the best two fit players play). Choose a short pre-Cup camp: training (a little better in the Cup, a little more tired) or recovery (fresh for the Cup).
- Offers wait on the Career page and the job market, with a notice on the Dashboard.

## The Empire Nations Cup, day by day
- **Played over four days**, after the last regular World Tour round: group day, quarter-finals, semi-finals, then medal day.
- **Watch any Cup match on the 3D court** on its day, from the Nations Cup schedule or the Dashboard: each nation in its own colours, with the Nations Cup boards round the court.
- **Play your own nation's matches in Manual or Auto.** Every other match is Auto.
- Matches you don't watch are decided at the end of their day. Medals, trophies and Club News work as before.

## Medal ceremonies
- **The World Finals and the Nations Cup end on the podium:** the gold, silver and bronze pairs on steps 1, 2 and 3, medals round their necks, waving, with a flag by every name and a big banner behind. Cup players wear their nation's colours, World Tour players their club's. It plays every time, and you can skip it.
- **World Finals bronze:** the losing semi-finalist higher on the World Tour ladder takes bronze (points, then wins, then sets, then points won and lost). Only if they are dead level is a third-place match played. Only the bronze pair's club gets the bronze in its Trophy Cabinet.
- **Continental leagues:** a gold, silver and bronze cup beside the top three once the ladder is final.

## AI clubs
- **AI clubs now scout a player before they bid for her**, and each runs at most 10 scouting reports a season, no more than 2 on players at any one club.
- **Each AI club makes at most 10 bids a season** (seniors and youths together), no more than 2 to any one club, yours included. A bid that is turned down still counts.

## Also
- **Real flags on every screen:** youth cards, player and staff cards, the Nations Cup pages, the job market and the Dashboard. No more letters in place of flags.
- **49 new staff faces:** 13 doctors, 12 registrars, 12 physiotherapists and 12 physio assistants, in the Staff and Medical Market. Every AI club can now find a doctor or physio.
